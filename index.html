A permissionless protocol, not a company.

Bazaar is the first ever fully permissionless derivatives marketplace, where perpetual futures on almost any asset with an on-chain price feed can be traded by anyone, against anyone. Built on Arbitrum, Bazaar is immutable and unowned. A marketplace for everything can only stay neutral if no one can say no.

What makes Bazaar different

On-chain order book

Orders are matched on-chain the moment they arrive. There's no hidden matching engine and no sequencer to trust — the contract decides who gets filled, and at what price.

Open listing

Anyone can list a market, with no cap on how many. Stocks, crypto, commodities, FX — and the long-tail assets major perp exchanges skip. If it has an on-chain price feed, it can trade here.

Super-low fees

In calm, healthy markets, makers and takers each pay just 0.5 bp — 50 cents on a $10,000 trade. Only taker fees rise, and only when a market turns risky.

Built for real-world assets

Stocks and FX don't trade around the clock. Bazaar markets keep running through closures, and cash-settle cleanly after a merger, split, delisting or other major corporate action.

Censorship resistant

No company and no protocol-level sequencer stand between you and the market. Nobody can block your order, push it to the back of the line, or take the market offline.

Immutable

No admin keys, no upgrades, no multisig. The code that's deployed is the protocol — forever.

No governance

No DAO, no token voting, no committees. It runs on math and incentives, not politics.

Risk sets itself

No committee or governance sets leverage or fees. Each market measures its own risk and adjusts its margin requirements, insurance threshold and fees automatically.

Isolated insurance

Every market has its own insurance fund to keep winning traders whole in extreme volatility. Independent insurers choose which markets to back, so losses in one market never spill into another.

Every role is open

List a market, run a bot, back the insurance fund, build a frontend. Every job the exchange needs is open to anyone, and each one is rewarded.

Trading on Bazaar

No account, no gas, no closing bell.

No account

The contracts have no sign-up, KYC or region block — any wallet with USDC on Arbitrum can trade. Individual frontends and integrators may set their own rules.

No ETH needed

Sign your trades and let a relayer submit them, so USDC is the only thing you ever need to trade.

Markets you won't find elsewhere

Anyone can list, so long-tail assets the major perp exchanges skip get markets too — alongside stocks, FX and indices that trade around the clock.

Isolated collateral

Each market holds its own collateral, so a blowup in one can never touch your balance in another.

Run by independent operators, not a team

Six independent roles keep the protocol running. These roles are open to anyone. Everyone earns.

01

Integrators

Build frontends, APIs, bots and tools on top of the protocol.

02

Listers

Propose any asset with a live price feed as a new market.

03

Insurers

Deposit into a market's insurance fund and earn a share of its fees. In return you take the first loss when liquidations fall short.

04

Liquidators

Close out accounts that fall below their margin.

05

Keepers

Run a bot that fires stop orders, sells off liquidated positions and keeps prices fresh.

06

ADL Bots

When losses outgrow a market's insurance fund, auto-deleveraging closes the most profitable positions against them to keep the market solvent.
