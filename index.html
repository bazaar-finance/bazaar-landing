What is Bazaar

A permissionless protocol, not a company.

Bazaar is the first ever fully permissionless derivatives marketplace where perpetual futures on almost any asset with a price can be traded by anyone, against anyone. Bazaar is immutable and unowned. A marketplace for everything can only stay neutral if no one can say no.

What makes Bazaar different

Open listing

Almost any asset with a live oracle feed can become a market. Stocks, crypto, commodities, FX, international equities — listed by anyone.

Censorship resistant

Matching is run by independent sequencers that compete for fees — and are penalized for censoring orders.

No house counterparty

Every trade matches trader against trader on an on-chain order book. Liquidity providers never take the other side of your position.

Every role is open

Run a sequencer, hunt liquidations, challenge fraud, underwrite risk. Every job the exchange needs is permissionless — and pays.

Immutable

No upgrades, no proxy patterns, no multisig. The deployed code is the protocol — forever.

No governance

No DAO, no token voting, no committees. It runs on math and incentives, not politics.

Run by independent operators, not a team

Six independent roles keep the protocol running. These roles are open to anyone. Everyone earns.

01

Integrators

Build frontends, APIs, relayer bots, and tools on top of the protocol. Earn a share of trading fees on every order routed through them.

02

Sequencers

Post bonds to match orders on chain and earn per-fill fees. Anyone with a bond can sequence.

03

Watchtowers

Challenge sequencers that partake in censorship and skip orders.

04

Insurers

Deposit capital into the per-asset insurance fund and earn yield from liquidations and trading fees; absorb losses from bad debt on liquidations.

05

Liquidators

Liquidate underwater positions before they become insolvent.

06

ADL Bots

Execute auto-deleveraging when liquidation exposure exceeds the insurance fund. Close winning positions against pending liquidations via a Dutch auction.
